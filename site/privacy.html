Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Logoxels is a logo design app for Android made by Pixels Limited, a company in Nigeria ("Pixels", "we"). This policy covers the app and this website, logoxels.pixelslimited.ng.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind in the app.
  • Your designs and images stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app goes online only when you tap Check for updates.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your current design. Logoxels autosaves it in the app's private storage so it is there when you return. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.
  • Files you save or export. They go only where you choose, using Android's file picker.
  • Images you trace. The app reads the single image you pick and converts it to shapes on your phone. It is not uploaded.
  • Downloaded updates. An update file is kept in the app's cache until Android or you clear it.

When the app uses the internet

Only when you choose File > Check for updates. The app then contacts GitHub, where the releases are published (api.github.com, and github.com for the file download). GitHub necessarily sees your IP address and basic request details, and handles them under its own privacy statement. The app sends no account, device identifier or design data. We receive nothing from this request.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetTo check for and download updates when you ask.
Install appsSo Android can install a downloaded update after you tap Install. You can switch this off in Android settings at any time.

Logoxels does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage.

This website

The site is plain pages with no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and operation. The Download button takes you to GitHub.

Children

Logoxels collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about app users, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold data about you, for example from contacting us, you may ask to see it, correct it or have it deleted under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
