Design logos on your phone.

Logoxels is a vector logo editor for Android. Free, works offline and needs no account.

Download for AndroidHow to install

Android 8.0 or newer. Not on Google Play yet.

What you can do

True corner rounding

Round any corner on any shape or path, with exact curves instead of approximations.

Shapes and pen

Rectangles, ellipses, polygons, stars and lines stay editable. Draw free paths with the pen and edit individual nodes.

Sketch recognition

Draw a circle, rectangle, triangle, star or line by hand and Logoxels straightens it into a clean shape.

Combine and cut

Union, subtract, intersect and exclude. Cut with the knife or scissors, group, clip and arrange layers.

Text that bends

Type on a path, inside a shape or warp it into an arch, wave or flag. Convert text to outlines.

Gradients and strokes

Linear and radial gradients, opacity, dashed lines and your choice of caps and joins.

Image trace

Turn a photo or sketch into smooth vector shapes, entirely on your phone.

Precision tools

Grid, snapping, guides and rulers, rotate and flip without losing a shape's live properties.

Export anywhere

SVG, PNG with transparency, PDF, layered PSD, EPS and AI, plus a project file you can reopen.

Install

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Tap Install. Later, open File > Check for updates inside Logoxels to get new versions.
